VigilExplainable multi-hazard disaster prediction

An AI-based early-warning platform that forecasts seven natural-hazard types across 25 Indian cities with XGBoost and LightGBM ensembles, explained with SHAP, and served through FastAPI and PostgreSQL. The research paper, Multi-Hazard Disaster Prediction and Alert System Using Real-Time Environmental Data, is accepted in ISJEM.

The problem

Disaster response needs forecasts that people can act on and trust. A risk score without an explanation is hard to defend in an operations room, so explainability was a requirement from the start, not an add-on.

What I built

  • Per-hazard models: local ML models for flood, cyclone, drought, heatwave and compound risk, an API-driven wildfire estimator on NASA FIRMS data, and a rule-based hurricane estimator.
  • XGBoost and LightGBM ensembles with SHAP explanations surfaced next to every prediction.
  • Seasonal climatological bias correction: real-time weather features are corrected against monthly baselines before inference.
  • Confidence-weighted ensemble fusion for compound, cascading hazards.
  • A FastAPI + SQLAlchemy + PostgreSQL backend with JWT auth, and a React 18 + TypeScript dashboard with maps and charts.

Results

  • ROC AUC 0.74 to 0.88 across hazard types: heatwave 0.876, cyclone 0.852.
  • Coverage of 7 hazard types across 25 Indian cities.
  • Research paper accepted in ISJEM.
